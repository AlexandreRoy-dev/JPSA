Planned shutdowns

A shutdown is won before the first cover comes off. The window itself is not renegotiated.

What that means, in practice.

Plant shutdowns concentrate a year’s work into a few days, sometimes a few shifts. Preparation decides the result: sequence, parts, resources, safety, restart.

JPSA arrives with a crew sized for the window, not for the brochure. We execute, we hold the tempo, we return the line to service.

  • Shutdown preparation with your production leads
  • Mechanics deployed to the real load
  • Mechanical work executed in the planned sequence
  • Restart support and end-of-shutdown findings
